# cc1101

## Sleep and wake checks read from the chip (#22519)

`scripts/cc1101_sleep/`: `cc1101-sleep.yaml` runs an ESP32-S3 (ESP-IDF) with an E07-M1101D module on SPI and runs
tests T0 to T6 4 s after boot; `cctest.hpp` reads every config register and the PATABLE directly over SPI. The log
ends with `DONE fails=N`, and T6 prints whether each setter woke a sleeping chip.

- Proof that the chip sleeps: with CSn low, SO (MISO) stays high until the crystal runs, so it is high after
  `power_down()` and low at once when awake. PATABLE entries 1 to 7 are also lost in SLEEP, so write marks to them
  before `power_down()` and check that they are gone.
- Selecting the chip to check it wakes it, so after the check the chip is awake while the component still thinks it
  sleeps. Call `wake_up()` and `begin_rx()` before the next case.
- Leave FSCAL3 to FSCAL1 (0x23 to 0x25), FSTEST (0x29) and AGCTEST (0x2B) out of register comparisons: calibration
  and the chip itself change them.
- Results: on 7e6b90d08b, `set_frequency`, `set_channel` and `set_modulation_type` woke a sleeping chip and left it
  in RX. 9e65d0c327 fixed that; every other check passed on both, and 200 sleep and wake cycles took about 1 s.

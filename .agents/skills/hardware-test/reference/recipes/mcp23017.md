# MCP23017 IOCON and interrupt line (#22232)

Bench: an ESP8266 (Feather HUZZAH) with an MCP23017 breakout at 0x27, SDA GPIO4, SCL GPIO5, INTA to GPIO14, INTB to
GPIO13, PA0 jumpered to PB0 and PA1 to PB1, no extra pull-ups (the breakout's were enough at 100 kHz). The esptool
reset keeps the expander powered, so a reflash shows whether register bits from the previous build survive.

`scripts/mcp23017/`: the `mcptest` external component (`ext/mcptest`) reads IOCON at 0x0A and 0x0B 6 s after boot,
toggles PA0 and PA1 20 times, samples the INT levels right after and 200 ms after each write, counts INTB edges in
an interrupt, checks the PB binary sensors (`SUMMARY` line), then checks whether IOCONB aliases IOCONA
(`IOCONB_TEST`). `int.yaml` wires INTA to the hub's `interrupt_pin`, open drain or push-pull by the `open_drain`
substitution (variants `int-od` and `int-pp`); `no-int.yaml` has no interrupt pin and counts INTA edges in
`mcptest`. `run.sh VARIANT LABEL` (env `TREE`, `PORT`, `SC`, `PY`) builds, flashes and captures 14 s.

- In BANK=0, 0x0A and 0x0B are the same register: a write to one reads back from the other.
- A push-pull INT line works without a pull-up. Open drain with no pull-up stays low after the first interrupt
  (ESP8266 INPUT), so the hub loop never stops and the inputs still update by polling.
- Builds before #22232 leave ODR and MIRROR set across reflashes.

# Real BMI270 breakout

`scripts/bmi270_real/` for an ESP8266 (Feather HUZZAH, SDA GPIO4, SCL GPIO5): `common.yaml` package (bus, `imu.yaml`
with the hub and sensors) with `probe.hpp` (malloc count and free heap between on_boot 600.5 and 599.5, around the DATA
setups, and a raw dump of registers 0x00/02/03/21/40 to 43/4B/6B/7C/7D after setup and every 20 s), variants
`default.yaml`, `nondef.yaml` (16G, 1600 Hz, 125 DPS, 25 Hz, BMM150 aux), `xform.yaml` (axis map, calibrate_level then
clear_calibration, read with `phase.py`), `tcount.yaml` (temperature publish counter), `scan.yaml` (registers at 0x68
and 0x69), `run.sh YAML NAME PORT [SECONDS]` and `summ.py CAPTURE...` (setup lines, means, spread and magnitude). Used
on #22778, #22780, #22739 and #22107; the fake BMI270 for the same PRs is in [fake_i2c.md](fake_i2c.md).

- A four wire breakout with SDO and CS open straps the chip to 0x68 (0x69 NACKs). CHIP_ID 0x24; INTERNAL_STATUS reads
  0x00 before the config upload and 0x01 after it. With `aux_device: BMM150` and no BMM150 fitted, the aux chip ID
  reads 0x00 at 0x10 to 0x13 and ERR_REG reads 0x80 (aux error) afterwards.
- A board lying still but tilted reads a little off (0.03, 0.02, -0.99 g, gyro within 1 °/s of 0): compare PR and dev
  per axis, not against 1 g.
- The 8 KB config upload at the ESP8266 default bus speed takes about 2.7 s of setup (2.5 s with one INIT_ADDR write,
  #22778); dev made 256 + 11 mallocs in the setup (one per 32 byte burst).

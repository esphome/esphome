# Real TCA9548A / PCA9548A multiplexer (#22827, #22862)

`scripts/tca9548a_real/` for an ESP8266 (Feather HUZZAH, SDA GPIO4, SCL GPIO5) with a PCA9548A breakout at 0x70 (A0 to
A2 and RESET open) and a BMI270 on channel 0: `tscan.yaml` (root scan, then for each mux the control readback and a scan
per channel selected by hand), `tca.yaml` (hub plus `../bmi270_real/imu.yaml`, `../bmi270_real/probe.hpp` and
`twitrace.hpp`, which wraps `twi_writeTo` and `twi_readFrom` to record every transaction the Wire core sends during
setup, hash them and check the select, device, deselect order), `wdtrun.sh NAME FREQ [RESETS] [SECONDS]` (boots, Soft
WDT resets and full setups per reset). Build `tca.yaml` once with `../bmi270_real/run.sh`. The simulated mux is in
[i2c_slave.md](i2c_slave.md).

- The twi wrap is the cheap way to see the upstream bytes when the ESP8266 is itself the master; a wire sniffer needs a
  second board. The control register reads back the selected mask, and 0x00 after a deselect. The breakout carries
  its own pull ups.
- At the default 50 kHz the BMI270 8 KB config upload through the mux (780 channel operations, each with a select and a
  deselect) trips the ESP8266 soft watchdog (about 3 s) inside the Wire byte loop: a boot loop before #22862. Use
  `frequency: 400kHz` on the bus (setup then takes 0.47 s). A watchdog reset in the middle of a write can leave SDA
  low, and the next boot marks the tca9548a failed; the boot after that recovers.
- With one INIT_ADDR write (#22778) the upload at 50 kHz is 3.15 s and passes, so use 40 kHz to reproduce the loop
  (3 Soft WDT resets per 12 s on 10 of 10 boots); with `feed_wdt()` in the upload (#22862) 10 of 10 full setups at
  3.93 s.
- dev, #22780 and #22827 sent identical upstream bytes (2342 transactions, same hash); mallocs 265, 265 and 9.
